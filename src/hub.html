Everything you mark — rules, verbs, words, listening tasks, drill scores, training results, mock tests, drafts and mindmaps — is saved in this browser only, on this device. The Danish you hear on the website is recorded with Google's Chirp 3 HD Danish voices — six of them, a man's or a woman's voice where the sentence calls for one.
